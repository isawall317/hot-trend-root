Character reference sheet for a knowledge comic.

MAIN CHARACTER — "Xiao Yu" (小宇):
- 8-year-old East Asian boy, slimmer build, child proportions (1:4 head-to-body)
- Black straight hair, ear-length with a few bangs falling on forehead
- Round face, pale skin, soft features
- **Distinctive marks**: small mole at the outer end of the LEFT eyebrow; light brown birthmark (~2cm elongated) on the RIGHT wrist
- Wearing white-and-blue horizontal-stripe swim trunks (3-4 stripes, never change)
- Blue swimming goggles with white frame (always the same blue lens, white border)
- White skin tone, warm beige base

SUPPORTING CHARACTERS:

Coach: 45-year-old East Asian man, crew cut with grey at temples, silver whistle on a rope around neck, white polo shirt + dark blue swim trunks, solid/stoic expression

Older brother (flashback only): 15-year-old East Asian teen, slicked-back black hair, slim but taller than Xiao Yu, dark blue swim trunks, warm encouraging smile, similar eyes to Xiao Yu but slightly narrower

Classmates (background group): 3-4 children of varied skin tones, swim caps in different colors (red, white), one boy with a cap, one girl with ponytail, one chunky boy with red goggles

STYLE: ligne-claire comic art, clean uniform black outlines (2-3px), flat color fills, cel-shading hard shadows (no gradients). No grey tones, only bright saturated colors. Skin tones are warm beige.

REQUIREMENTS:
- Show Xiao Yu in 4 poses: front view, side view, 3/4 view, action pose (diving)
- Include swim goggles and trunks visible in every pose
- Mole on left eyebrow clearly visible in all close-up views
- White background with light grey grid (reference sheet layout)
- Label: "XIAO YU · 8 yo" at the bottom
- Reference sheet should be clean and identity-anchoring, NOT a full illustration
