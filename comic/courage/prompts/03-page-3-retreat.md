- Black ear-length hair, round face, pale skin
- Small mole at outer end of LEFT eyebrow
- Light brown birthmark on RIGHT wrist

PANEL 1: Medium shot — Xiao Yu finally standing at the very EDGE of the diving platform. Empty space ahead of him, vast blue sky behind. His arms hang stiffly at his sides. He's small against the empty expanse.

PANEL 3: Medium shot — three or four classmates at the pool edge waving and cheering up at the platform. One boy in a cap, one girl with ponytail, one chunky boy with red goggles. All smiles, mouths open shouting. Speech bubbles: "小宇加油！" "冲啊！" (Go Xiao Yu! Come on!)

Visual hook: The Coach and classmates form a visual "encirclement" creating psychological pressure. Xiao Yu is small and isolated in the upper space, while the supportive figures are clustered below.
